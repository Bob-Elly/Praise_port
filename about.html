About Me

Hi, I'm Godwin Praise

I am a passionate UI/UX and brand designer with a strong background in Mechatronics Engineering from the Federal University of Technology Owerri (FUTO). I combine analytical problem-solving with creative design execution to build intuitive, user-centered digital products that solve real-world problems.

Over the past few years, I have collaborated with cross-functional teams, startups, and clients across multiple countries to turn complex ideas into seamless digital experiences. Whether I am conducting user research, crafting scalable design systems, or prototyping interactive experiences, my goal is always to deliver designs that are aesthetically compelling, deeply functional, and aligned with measurable business outcomes.
