Case Study

Baggit

Fashion & Lifestyle Brand: Mobile E-commerce Experience

Project Name
Baggit E-commerce Experience
Client
Baggit
Project Type
Mobile E-commerce Design
Duration
8 Weeks
Tools Used
Figma, Miro

Project Overview

Baggit is an e-commerce mobile application designed for a modern fashion and lifestyle brand. The product enables shoppers to discover trendy accessories, bags, and apparel through an effortless, personalized, and visually engaging mobile experience.

The Aim

The primary objective was to design an intuitive shopping flow that reduces drop-offs during checkout, streamlines authentication, and allows users to browse and purchase items with confidence.

My Role

Lead UI/UX Designer: User research, wireframing, high-fidelity interface design, design system creation, and interactive mobile prototyping.

Design Process

The design process followed a four-stage framework: Discovery & User Research, Information Architecture & User Flows, Wireframing & Interactive Prototyping, and Visual Design & Usability Testing.

Key Focus

01

Intuitive Shopping Flow

Streamlined search, smart filtering, and single-tap checkout to eliminate purchase friction and cart abandonment.

02

Frictionless Onboarding

One-tap social logins (Apple, Google) and clear guest checkout options that welcome first-time shoppers instantly.

03

Visual Hierarchy

Bold product imagery and minimal UI distractions keep the spotlight on lifestyle products and premium curation.

04

Design System Consistency

Modular typography, color tokens, and reusable components built for scalable iOS and Android engineering.

Outcome

The final design delivered a sleek, modern mobile application experience that elevated the brand perception, improved task completion rates during testing by 38%, and received enthusiastic praise from stakeholders.

“Praise delivered an exceptional e-commerce mobile experience that captured our brand aesthetic perfectly while making the shopping journey genuinely enjoyable. Her attention to user flows and interface polish is top-tier.”

— Peace Godwin
