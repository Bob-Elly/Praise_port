Case Study

Fitness App

Personalized Workout & Progress Tracking

Project Name
Fitness App
Project Type
Mobile app

Project Overview

A mobile fitness app that builds personalized workouts around a user's body, goals and lifestyle, and keeps them motivated with streaks, XP, leagues and achievements. The design covers onboarding, a home screen, a workout summary and an analytics section with Overview, Trends and Goals views.

My Role

UI/UX design: onboarding, home, workout summary and analytics screens.

Key Focus

01

Motivation Built In

XP rewards, competitive bronze/silver leagues, streaks, and milestone badges that turn daily physical training into an engaging habit.

02

Progress at a Glance

Overview, Trends, and Goals tabs that transform complex activity data, calories, and body measurements into simple stats and clean charts.

03

Quick Access to Training

Featured top workouts (Squat Training, Push Ups) and a structured weekly schedule directly accessible from the home screen.

04

A Clear Post-Workout Summary

Immediate post-workout feedback highlighting perceived effort percentage, calories burned, duration, and completion badges in one consolidated view.

Application Screens Showcase

Engaging welcome onboarding screen setting personalized goals

Home feed with league ranking, streak counter, top exercises, and daily schedule

Post-workout summary reporting effort percentage, calories burned, and newly earned badges

Performance overview showing total workouts, streak records, time tracked, and muscle group breakdown

Weekly trends comparison and milestone tracking for multi-month health goals
